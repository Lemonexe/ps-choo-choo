THE LOCOMOTIVE BEHIND THE MODEL

01 118

A Pacific with a long working life.

Built in 1934, 01 118 is a preserved example of the DRG Class 01 express passenger locomotive: a two-cylinder, simple-expansion Pacific with 2 metre driving wheels and a 16 bar boiler. Its dimensions set the scale of pS choo choo, from the 600 × 660 mm cylinders to the 34 m³ water tender and 10 tonne coal bunker. This simulator borrows those proportions and uses simplified equilibrium thermodynamics to let you explore how firing, pressure, cutoff, and adhesion work together.
